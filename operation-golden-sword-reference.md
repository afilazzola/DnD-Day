# Operation Golden Sword — Player Quick Reference

> *"The goal is not to reclaim Amn in a single battle. The goal is to **break it**."*
> — War Council Directive

---

## The World

**Setting:** Forgotten Realms, Sword Coast, late 1480s–mid-1490s DR
**Character Level:** 7
**Staging Ground:** Velen, Dragon's Head Peninsula — a fortified coastal town packed with soldiers, rowboats, and quiet dread. This is where the invasion begins.

The **Kingdom of Amn** — one of the wealthiest powers on the Sword Coast — has fallen. Its capital, **Athkatla**, is occupied. Every land approach is sealed behind walls of steel and discipline. The only viable weakness is the coastline, and the only hope is a desperate, full-scale amphibious invasion.

---

## The True Objective

Strike fast. Break the Cathedral. Get out.

**Sainte-Mère-Église Cathedral** is the nerve centre of the entire occupation:

- A fortress commanding the inland road
- The primary arcane relay between Athkatla and the Iron Conclave's eastern armies
- The single point that coordinates all enemy forces on the coast

**If the Cathedral falls:**
- Eastern armies are cut off from command
- Battlefield intelligence collapses
- Athkatla becomes exposed
- The liberation of Amn becomes possible

**If the Cathedral holds:**
- The invasion will be crushed between converging armies
- The Iron Conclave responds with forces too vast to resist

This is not a battle for glory. **It is a race against consolidation.**

---

## The Enemy — The Iron Conclave

A militarized alliance of Duergar and Orc War-Clans. This is not a raiding force. **This is an occupation.**

### High Forgemaster Tharûm Blackmantle
*Duergar Warlord / Artificer / Warlock — Supreme Commander*

Rose from the brutal politics of the Underdark, where survival demanded calculation over strength. After his clan was abandoned during a territorial collapse, he devoted himself to siegecraft, logistics, and the economics of war — until domination became a matter of planning rather than chance.

He chose Amn not out of hatred, but **efficiency**. Its wealth, guilds, and trade networks are the perfect engine for continental conquest. He wages war as an industry. He does not seek glory — only stability enforced by overwhelming advantage.

> *"The clans fight because they must. The forges burn because they profit. I rule because the system functions."*

### The Warmagus of Sainte-Mère-Église
*Conclave Archmage / Strategic Commander — Cathedral Commander*

Before the Iron Conclave, the Warmagus was one of Athkatla's most beloved arcane scholars — a specialist in **communication magic**, not war magic. Their life's work was a simple belief: most conflicts come from ignorance, and if information could flow freely between people, violence could be prevented before it began. They taught. They built relay networks for merchants and diplomats. They wanted to connect the world.

When Tharûm's forces surrounded Athkatla, they stayed — believing their skills could broker a negotiated surrender and limit the slaughter. Tharûm listened, then showed them the alternative: a detailed operational plan for what would happen if the city resisted. Street by street. Quarter by quarter. He didn't threaten. He simply presented the mathematics.

The Warmagus negotiated the city's surrender. Athkatla did not burn. Its people are alive. **And that is the chain around their neck.**

Every cooperation that followed was justified the same way: *if I stop, thousands die.* The skills they built to bring people together are the same skills that now make the Iron Conclave function. They told themselves they were managing it. Containing it. They weren't wrong. They also weren't right enough to matter.

The forbidden contingency — fracturing their consciousness into multiple autonomous arcane aspects — was not purely a military decision. It was **dissociation**. The weight of what they had become was too much for a single mind to carry. By shattering into fragments, no piece has to look at the whole picture at once.

*They still remember what they were building. They just can't find the way back.*

### The Factions

| Faction | Role | Methods |
|---|---|---|
| **Blackmantle Duergar** | Engineers, commanders, overseers | Enslaved fire elementals, shadow-forged weapons, rigid formations |
| **Orc War-Clans** | Shock troops, vanguard, enforcers | Brutal frontal assaults, boarding actions, overwhelming numbers |

- Duergar are bound by **contracts and ideology**
- Orcs are bound by **fear, momentum, and promised plunder**
- Neither trusts the other — both fear Tharûm's reprisal
- The Cathedral exists to ensure neither side can rebel without being crushed by the other

### What You'll Face on the Beach
- Duergar infantry, engineers, and siege overseers
- Orc berserkers and shock troops
- The "Amnian Wall" — fortified beach emplacements and anti-landing barricades
- Arcane artillery positions
- **Aerial:** Wyvern cavalry, Duergar gyrocopters, war-mage spellcasters, shadow-forged bombers

## The Four Acts

### Act I — The Eve of War *(Velen)*
Preparation. The town is overrun with soldiers, mercenaries, and quiet fear. Buy your supplies. Say your goodbyes. Once the invasion begins, there is no returning to Velen.

### Act II — The Iron Tide *(The Landing)*
Four companies hit simultaneously across separate theaters. The invasion begins at H-Hour: dawn. Your table's success or failure shapes the conditions every other table faces.

### Act III — The Bridge *(Pegasus Bridge, Orn River)*
All four theaters converge on a single point: **Pegasus Bridge** over the Orn River. It is the only crossing capable of supporting siege engines and massed troops. Intelligence confirms the Iron Conclave has rigged it for demolition.

If the bridge falls, the invasion stalls. **Everything depends on it.**

### Act IV — The Fall of the Cathedral *(Final Battle)*
All surviving forces storm Sainte-Mère-Église Cathedral. As the assault begins, the Warmagus reveals their contingency: fracturing into **four arcane aspects**, each tied to a theater of war, each manifesting near a different company. Victory requires defeating all — or a decisive majority — simultaneously.

When the last aspect falls: arcane relays collapse, signal beacons fail, orders cease, formations fragment. The Iron Conclave has not been destroyed — **but its grip on Amn has been broken.**

## Theater Objectives

### T1 — Iron Wolves (General Assault)
**Act II:** Storm Juno Beach — break shoreline defenses, draw enemy attention, reach the road toward the Orn River
**Act III:** Break the Line — breach fortified positions, clear the road for the siege train, link up with Bête Noire at Pegasus Bridge
**Secondary:** Destroy barricades, kill/rout commanders, capture signal towers
**If they fail:** Enemy pressure spikes everywhere; Siege Train gets bogged down; bridge reinforcements arrive from the beach side

### T2 — Stone Breakers (Siege Train)
**Act II:** Land catapults and ballistas at Gold Beach under cover of the main assault; protect them from enemy fire
**Act III:** Haul massive siege engines up the sandy bluffs of Hoc's Point using winches, magic, beasts of burden, or brute force — then cross Pegasus Bridge to the Cathedral
**Key tension:** The engines are slow and vulnerable. Losing them is catastrophic.
**If they fail:** Cathedral becomes nearly impregnable; Act IV shifts to brutal attrition; casualties spike

### T3 — Bête Noire (Portals & Bridges)
**Entry:** Teleported behind enemy lines to a resistance-held watchtower
**Act II:** Eliminate enemy patrols before the alarm is raised; establish a foothold; move toward the bridge
**Act III:** Assault the Duergar demolition platoon, neutralize sappers, disarm explosive runes and barrels, hold the bridge until reinforcements arrive
**If they fail:** Bridge destroyed — invasion stalls; siege engines cannot cross; Cathedral fortifies

### T4 — Storm Riders (Sky Control)
**Act II:** Achieve air superiority — intercept wyvern cavalry, gyrocopter bombers, war-mage spellcasters; protect ground forces from aerial bombardment
**Act III:** Land on the inland road past the bridge; establish a blocking position against Cathedral reinforcements; hold until beach forces break through
**If they fail:** Air strikes harass all theaters; Cathedral reinforcements reach the bridge; Bête Noire gets overwhelmed

### How It All Connects

| Theater | Act III Role | If They Fail |
|---|---|---|
| T1 Iron Wolves | Break through to the bridge | Siege Train trapped, link-up fails |
| T2 Stone Breakers | Haul engines up Hoc's Point | Cathedral becomes nearly impregnable |
| T3 Bête Noire | Disarm the bridge demolitions | Bridge destroyed, invasion stalls |
| T4 Storm Riders | Block reinforcements from Cathedral | Portal team overwhelmed at bridge |

**No theater stands alone.**

# The War Board — Multi-Table Mechanics

Each table runs independently, but shares Act-level consequences, global events, and cross-table actions. The **War Board** tracks the overall battle state — visible to everyone throughout the event. When your table succeeds, everyone sees it. When the enemy pushes back, everyone feels it.

---

## War Momentum Tokens

Tokens are awarded at major Act-level objectives — not individual dice rolls. Outcomes should be graduated where possible (avoid hard pass/fail).

| Outcome | War Track |
|---|---|
| Major success (e.g., both siege engines landed) | +2 |
| Partial success (e.g., one engine landed) | +1 |
| Failure (e.g., no engines landed) | −1 |

- **Positive tokens** → War Track moves toward Allied Advantage
- **Negative tokens** → War Track moves toward Enemy Advantage
- Track starts at **0**

---

## War Track Thresholds

| Level | Effect |
|---|---|
| **+4** | Enemy morale breaks — reduced resistance across all theaters |
| **+2** | Allied reinforcements arrive, granting temporary aid |
| 0 | Neutral |
| **−2** | Artillery begins firing periodically at all theaters |
| **−4** | Elite enemy units appear in encounters |
| **−6** | Warmagus begins pre-channeling arcane energy |

> *Negative momentum increases tension but should never make victory impossible.*

---

## Periodic Global Events

Every **45–60 minutes**, the Lead DM triggers a Global Battlefield Event affecting all theaters simultaneously.

**Favorable War State events:**
- Allied cleric wave grants temporary *Bless*
- Rebel sabotage weakens enemy defenses
- Enemy reinforcements delayed

**Poor War State events:**
- Coordinated artillery strike hits a random table
- Wyvern air raid strafes the battlefield
- Elite shock troops arrive as reinforcements

---

## Signal Actions

**Once per Act**, your table may perform a Signal Action to directly help another theater. No physical movement required.

**How to signal:**
- Send a magical *Sending* message
- Fire an arcane signal flare
- Activate a relay beacon

**What it can do:**
- Grant advantage to another table
- Cancel one incoming artillery event
- Add +1 to the War Track
- Delay enemy reinforcements

**The cost:** Signal Actions aren't free. Your table might lose a bonus objective, burn a rare resource, or take −1 on the War Track. **Choose wisely.**

---

## Final Battle Impact (Act IV)

The difficulty of the assault on the Warmagus is directly shaped by the final War Track position.

**Negative War Track** — each point below 0 makes the Warmagus stronger:
- +15 HP per point
- +1 Legendary Resistance per point
- Additional battlefield abilities

**Positive War Track** — each point above 0 weakens the Warmagus:
- Abilities removed
- Maximum HP reduced
- Legendary Actions stripped

> *Every objective matters. Every point on the War Track counts. When you face the Warmagus, you'll know exactly how well — or poorly — Operation Golden Sword went.*

---

## The Warmagus — Act IV Mechanic

On assault, the Warmagus **fractures into four arcane aspects**:

| Aspect | What It Truly Represents |
|---|---|
| **Control** | The belief they could manage this — keep it contained, prevent the worst |
| **Destruction** | The violence done in their name, the deaths they told themselves were necessary |
| **Deception** | The lies told to themselves and to the people of Athkatla who trusted them |
| **Dominion** | The power they allowed to grow until they could no longer imagine leaving it behind |

Each aspect manifests near one theater and must be defeated independently. **Victory is collective** — all aspects (or a decisive majority) must fall. When they do, the arcane relay collapses, the signal network fails, and the Iron Conclave's grip on Amn breaks.

The question players will carry into Act IV is not whether the Warmagus is evil. It is whether evil is something you *become* — or something that happens to you when you run out of better options.
